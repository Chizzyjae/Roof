Lead work and lead welding

Lead is one of the best and oldest roofing materials used today. It has a proven track record as a reliable and long-lasting option, acting as a natural sealant when applied. In addition, it is highly flexible and resistant to corrosion from UV rays and pollution, making it perfect for roofs in coastal areas and the north of England.

However, even the great British weather can weaken the toughest materials over time. As a result, Lead can split and fail, allowing water into a property. That’s where we come in.

Few people realise that Lead is recyclable, and there have been instances where we’ve used existing material to help patch up leaks. We can repair, replace or install new Lead on any roofing project – with all lead work welded and custom-made to fit your requirements.

Fascias, guttering and cladding

Your home's roof is the primary defence against the elements, but its fascias', soffits and gutters are often forgotten. Required for adequate drainage, they ensure excess water is never left standing. Fascias and soffits protect the property where the roof meets the exterior walls, while gutters help direct the flow into ground-level drains.

Blocked downpipes and gutters can lead to water ingress through walls which, over time, can result in expensive and necessary repairs.

It is therefore essential to keep these clear of debris, and where storm-damaged, repair or replace connections or guttering.

Additionally, fascias', soffits, and barges stop birds and insects from nesting in your roof space. It is therefore vital that these are maintained with servicing at least twice a year.

Ultimately, these crucial roof parts help your property stand out and ensure that rainwater ends where intended.

Whether you need broken or aged fittings replaced with UPVC or want cast iron finishings complementing the style of your roof, we work with all types of gutterings, fascias', soffits, and barges to protect your home from any potential damage.

Masonry repairs pointing and cement work

Crumbling cement work and ageing pointing can encourage water ingress to filter through stacks, ridges or brickwork. Pointing is the finish between the bricks or stonework of your property and the term used when repairing mortar joints. Mortar joints are the lungs of the wall – they allow water within the structure to enter and leave freely. If water leaves through brick or stone, it slowly disintegrates.

Roof bedding and pointing are an essential part of a roof's structure. The bedding holds the roof tiles in place, while the pointing creates a stronger hold and secondary tighter seal.

Both require maintenance over time. Aside from helping to hold up your house and protect against water ingress, mortar helps prevent heat loss through the walls. If left untreated, water can freeze in the bricks and surrounding mortar, which expands and causes damage. This freeze-thaw weathering can ultimately lead to a damp and cold house.

Fortunately, as masonry repair experts, we can quickly resolve these issues. After a simple visit, we'll make recommendations and explain how we'll fix the situation before they become an expensive problem.

Roof ventilation and condensation control

Your roof keeps bad weather out but also lets your property breathe. Your loft is a barrier that keeps heat in the rooms below while allowing air to enter the eaves and exit through ridge or tile vents. Not enough insulation, and your home will be noticeably colder. On the other hand, too much or incorrectly laid insulation (where it blocks the eaves) will result in condensation in the loft and rooms below.

Either way, the result is a property that becomes more expensive than it needs to be – through higher heating costs or repairs to fix dampness caused by excess air moisture.

We understand the importance of your loft as a thermal barrier to your home. Where ventilation is poor, we can advise on the correct positioning and installation of vents, and where insulation is required, we can ensure heat is not lost.

A small investment in the correct type of ventilation with well-placed insulation can make all the difference. So give us a call, and we'll save you money by keeping your home warm and condensation at bay.
